Understanding Family Planning

Family planning helps individuals and couples make informed decisions about pregnancy, child spacing, and reproductive health using safe and suitable contraceptive methods.

💊

Pills

Oral contraceptive pills taken daily to help prevent pregnancy safely and effectively.

💉

Injectables

Long-acting contraceptive injections that provide pregnancy protection for weeks or months.

🌸

Implants

Small flexible rods inserted under the skin to provide long-term pregnancy prevention.

🩺

IUCD

A small device inserted into the uterus for long-lasting and reversible contraception.

🛡️

Condoms

Barrier protection that helps prevent pregnancy while also reducing the risk of infections.

📅

Natural Methods

Fertility awareness and cycle tracking methods used for natural pregnancy planning.

Why Family Planning Matters

✔ Helps with healthy child spacing
✔ Supports safer motherhood
✔ Allows better postpartum recovery
✔ Reduces unintended pregnancies
✔ Promotes reproductive health awareness
✔ Helps families plan for the future

Every woman’s body and reproductive health needs are different. Professional guidance can help you choose the safest and most suitable family planning option.
